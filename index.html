Teerth Realties Owner News: Meet Founder Vijay Raundal

Vijay Raundal is the Founder and Managing Director of Teerth Realties. His professional journey began in 1986 and reflects years of experience across construction, infrastructure, and real estate.

Before establishing Teerth Realties, he gained experience in marble polishing, earthmoving machinery, and government contracting. He later established Vijay Constructions and worked across infrastructure and civil construction activities. These experiences helped shape his understanding of land, development, and project execution.

In 2008, he established Teerth Realties with a focus on residential and commercial development. His leadership emphasizes strategic planning, location selection, execution, and long-term value.
